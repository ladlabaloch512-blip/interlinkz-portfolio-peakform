About PeakForm Fitness

A mid size studio built for people who felt out of place in big, loud gyms.

Our story

PeakForm Fitness was built for people who felt out of place in big, loud gyms. We are a mid size studio with good equipment, honest prices and coaches who actually coach.

Our floor has a full free weights area, modern cardio machines, a dedicated class studio and clean changing rooms. Members range from first time gym goers to competitive lifters, and the rule of the house is simple: everyone is here to get better, and everyone is treated with respect while they do it.

Equipment and floor areas

  • Full free weights area with barbells and dumbbells
  • Modern cardio machines, including bikes for Spin Ride
  • A dedicated class studio for group sessions
  • Personal training area for one to one coaching
  • Clean changing rooms with lockers and showers

How we work

Start where you are

No body shame and no shouting. Beginners and competitive lifters train side by side, and everyone is treated with respect.

Honest prices

Clear plans, month to month, with every price on this website. Pause or cancel with one message.

Real coaching

Our coaches actually coach. Form checks, starter programmes and plan tweaks are part of your membership, not an extra.
